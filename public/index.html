Hello, I’m Max.

Great to see you, I am Max. My legal name is Jinghan Lan (兰景涵). I study at the University of Chicago as an undergraduate and major in Computer Science and Physics. I’m a member of the Existential Risk Laboratory (XLab) and Quantum Society, where I concentrate in AI security and Quantum Computing.

I enjoy building products that tackle specific issues in people’s lives. Some of my projects include CollegeDeck, an AI-agent-powered interface for autonomous college applications, Archstone, an alumni map website for resource connection, and Quarterly, a personal agent at UChicago that helps students handle logistics and planning.

Some fields that I wish to participate in the future include AI security, world models, physical AI, and quantum computing.

If you want to hear more about my story, you are welcome to check my blog posts.

  • Brief Discussion on the Transition to Physics and CSA conception of what would sustain me in life.November 13, 2026 · 3 min readRead →

    Most who know me would tell you in shock of my transition. They’d recall that I was an active debater of Politics in high school, and studied history and economics rigorously. Back then I wanted to be an economist. I liked Hayek and Friedman. I admired Deng Xiaoping and Lee Kuan Yew. Within a year, however, I’ve pulled myself from a history student with little mathematics background to a Physics student participating in frontier labs at the university. Of course, I wouldn’t stop here.

    I made the choice because I realized that economics and humanities lacked the real technical mastery and knowledge I needed to become a competent participant of the future. By that time, 2025, the first large language models were transitioning into autonomous agents. I cared about how these next-generation systems can be built. I admired how powerful they were, and I worried about the misuse of power that they may unleash, blocking self-realization with mass surveillance. I knew that Artificial Intelligence, as much as it empowers the individual to take on challenges, empowers existing authorities to tighten control and centralize power.

    I knew I needed to change my perspectives of the world if I wished to brace myself for Black Swans in the future. There is no best way to do that other than to put my skin in the game: to learn and build frontier technologies by myself. To create my own autonomy and prosperity, and hence have the slightest chance of shaping the world, I was compelled to act.

    By that time, I started to see my future in entrepreneurship, in using my own abilities and competence to create value that a part of society wanted. If I had the competence to establish my own business, I thought, I would no longer need to be coerced into working or complying to a large corporation. I would be less vulnerable to radical changes. I would have the negotiating power to deal with all the chaos of the world, and not have myself subject to the whim of others.

    This realization came to me while I was building one of my initial AI projects, the Weimar Pendulum, a 90-minute immersive history musical I directed in high school with the help of musical AI models. Despite having to coordinate chaotic details and deal with many breakdowns, I never sensed more freedom and fulfillment seeing it come to play. I discovered I could envision something big, then take it down, and execute it, something I did not dare to imagine before. And the end of that is something tremendous, with companionship built on the way.

    To slay or be slain; he who has chosen the former has made peace with the latter. After that, I’ve set my mind: I pay the price for all that I choose to pursue and bear. I don’t demand anything from anyone around me.

    And so I begin, with the basics of Math, Computer Science and Physics. I started to learn the basics of proofwriting and mathematical thinking, and the statistical modeling that seeks to understand life. On the road I discovered quantum computing, a hardcore technology, like AI, that is underdeveloped but if put through, can create tremendous value for improving living standards. I decided that it was going to be my next domain. I would absorb the technology and build companies.

    At the end of the day I hope to become an investor. That is not only investing in the financial sense, but more about fundamental value investing. Investing in kindness of the human spirit and excellence in action — all those valuable things. I invest with the intention of giving back, for I am a human just like all of us, and bear the same weaknesses, flaws, and glories which define humanity, yet among the most difficult times I have received invaluable kindness from those around me, who transcend personal feelings to give help and assistance.

    For all whose lives I’ve hurt because of my rashness and lack of human touch, I’m deeply sorry. I shall continue to tear down and rebuild, for the best outcome that will come.

    Max LanNovember 13, 2026
